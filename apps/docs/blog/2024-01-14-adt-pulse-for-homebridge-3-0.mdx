ADT Pulse for Homebridge 3.0.0 is a ground-up rewrite. It runs on a new TypeScript codebase, connects to the Canada ADT Pulse portal as well as the United States one, and adds a switch for silencing an alarm that rings while the system is disarmed.

{/* truncate */}

## Why It Matters

Version 2 lived in a single JavaScript file and only knew the United States portal. ADT Pulse customers in Canada had no way into the Home app, and every portal change meant working through one large file.

Version 3.0.0 came out of 24 public betas, starting December 16, 2023, with testing and hints from the community.

## What's Included

### Two Portal Regions

The new `subdomain` setting points the plugin at `portal` for the United States or `portal-ca` for Canada.

### A Modular Codebase

The plugin is split into accessory, API, detection, platform, and schema modules, written in TypeScript.

### Alarm Ringing Switch

A carbon monoxide or smoke alarm can ring while the system is disarmed. A dedicated panel switch silences it from the Home app.

### More Sensor States

Sensor statuses reported by the community are supported, and a sensor's **Trouble** status is treated as a tamper.

### Diagnostics

The `test-api` and `repl` scripts test and debug the ADT Pulse portal from the command line.
