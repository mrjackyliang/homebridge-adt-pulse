Convert a temperature <Terminology title="a portal device declared in your config" to="/docs/reference/terminology#sensor">sensor</Terminology>'s binary normal or abnormal status into degree values through the <Terminology title="HomeKit Accessory Protocol" to="/docs/reference/terminology#hap">HAP</Terminology> protocol so it can appear in the Home app.

## Why the Mapping Exists

The Temperature Sensor (`temperature`) processes <Terminology title="a portal device declared in your config" to="/docs/reference/terminology#sensor">sensor</Terminology> statuses differently from standard contact <Terminology title="a portal device declared in your config" to="/docs/reference/terminology#sensor">sensors</Terminology>.

A typical contact <Terminology title="a portal device declared in your config" to="/docs/reference/terminology#sensor">sensor</Terminology> conveys an open or closed status. The temperature <Terminology title="a portal device declared in your config" to="/docs/reference/terminology#sensor">sensor</Terminology> exposed in the Home app uses the <Terminology title="HomeKit Accessory Protocol" to="/docs/reference/terminology#hap">HAP</Terminology> protocol, which operates with temperature values rather than open or closed states.

To bridge that difference, the accessory converts the <Terminology title="a portal device declared in your config" to="/docs/reference/terminology#sensor">sensor</Terminology>'s binary status into a corresponding temperature in degrees.

## Status to Degree Mapping

The plugin maps each status to a fixed temperature value:

| Status   | Reading |
|----------|---------|
| Normal   | `0` C   |
| Abnormal | `100` C |

A reading of `0` C indicates a normal status, and a reading of `100` C indicates an abnormal status. These are the only two values the accessory reports.

## Adding a Temperature Sensor

A temperature <Terminology title="a portal device declared in your config" to="/docs/reference/terminology#sensor">sensor</Terminology> is exposed in read-only mode and must be listed explicitly in your configuration. Set its `adtType` to `temperature` in the <Terminology title="a portal device declared in your config" to="/docs/reference/terminology#sensor">sensor</Terminology> object:

```json title="config.json"
{
  "sensors": [
    {
      "name": "Basement Temperature",
      "adtName": "Basement Temperature Sensor",
      "adtZone": 12,
      "adtType": "temperature"
    }
  ]
}
```

The `adtName` and `adtZone` values must match the "Name" and "Zone" columns shown under the <Terminology title="the portal's tab listing sensor name, zone, and type" to="/docs/reference/terminology#system-tab">"System" tab</Terminology> of the ADT Pulse <Terminology title="ADT Pulse Web Portal, the plugin's data source" to="/docs/reference/terminology#portal">portal</Terminology>.
